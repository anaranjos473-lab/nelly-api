# NELLY-FISCAL P1 - Professional Readiness Package

## Purpose

Prepare a review package for a Mexican tax professional without converting assumptions, reference rates or technical classifications into definitive fiscal rules.

## Package contents

- `FiscalCase` contract and sample evidence.
- `FinancialEvent` contract and idempotency behavior.
- Ledger references and reconciliation results.
- Settlement snapshot boundary and known limitations.
- CFDI lifecycle and matching controls.
- Fiscal rule statuses and version metadata.
- Exception policy with no automatic correction.
- Fiscal RBAC permissions and authorization audit.
- Historical append-only audit trail and integrity verification.
- Reproducible test command and certification status.

## Professional questions

The reviewer must document a decision, rule version, effective period and supporting evidence for:

1. Nelly's tax regime and economic role.
2. Ownership of subtotal, delivery fee, tips, commission and third-party money.
3. Revenue recognition for Nelly, commerce and driver flows.
4. IVA, ISR and withholding implications by scenario.
5. CFDI issuer, receiver, concepts, payment method and cancellation policy.
6. Refunds, cancellations, reversals and partial adjustments.
7. Cash, electronic and mixed-payment reconciliation.
8. B2B commerce and required documentation.
9. Record retention and evidence requirements.
10. Required changes before production and controlled pilot approval.

## Boundary

Until this package is approved, fiscal rules remain `PENDING_VALIDATION`, fiscal cases remain `NOT_CERTIFIED`, and NELLY-FISCAL remains read-only. NELLY-ACCOUNTING is intentionally not started.
